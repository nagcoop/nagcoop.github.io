Privacy Policy

Last updated October 21, 2026

Who we are

Nagcoop (nagcoop.github.io) is a gaming website with articles, news, tutorials and a web emulator. This page explains what data is collected when you visit and how it is used.

Data we collect

The site has no sign-up and does not ask for your personal data. The following is handled automatically:

  • Browsing data, such as IP address, browser type, pages visited and time of access, recorded by our hosting and advertising providers.
  • Emulator files: ROMs and BIOS files you load into the emulator are processed only in your browser. They are never sent to any server. A BIOS you save stays only in your browser's local storage and can be removed at any time with the Remove button.
  • Newsletter: if you enter your email, it is used only to send site updates, and you can ask for removal at any time.

Cookies and advertising

This site displays ads from Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.

Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.

You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at www.aboutads.info. To learn more, see how Google uses information from partner sites.

Visitor statistics

We use GoatCounter to count visits. It does not use cookies and does not collect personal data: it records only the page visited, the referring site, browser and screen size, and the country derived from the IP address, which is not stored. The total number of visits may be shown publicly on the site.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log technical access data such as IP addresses for security purposes. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Third-party links and content

News shown on the home page links to the original stories on other websites, and videos come from YouTube. These services have their own privacy policies.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, laws such as the GDPR (European Union), the CCPA (California) and the LGPD (Brazil) give you the right to request access to, correction of or deletion of your data. To make a request, contact us through our YouTube channel.

Changes

This policy may be updated. The date of the latest update is always shown at the top of the page.
